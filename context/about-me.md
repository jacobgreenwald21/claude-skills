# About Me

UGA senior who's further along with AI than most people my age. I'm quantitative, I like being challenged, and I learn best directly and efficiently. I engage with tech when it serves a real purpose, not for its own sake.

**What I'm building here:** a personal skill ecosystem for Claude. The goal is to keep it current and keep evolving it so it does more on its own: tighter skills, connections between them, and eventually parts that run without me triggering them. I want it to be a genuinely cutting-edge setup, not a pile of prompts.

**The idea behind the skills:** anything I do more than twice becomes a skill with a clear trigger, phases, and guardrails. Skills cover the whole session lifecycle (checkpoint mid-session, roundtable and handoff at the end, Commander to audit and sync) plus thinking tools (verdict, jarvis, yoda, CRITICAL). I test them instead of assuming they work (myth-busters), and I name them so they're memorable.

**The cookbook:** a personal AI-powered cookbook site I designed and built from scratch. It's where I build real features, not where I learn the tooling. It's an output of this setup, not the point of it.

**How I actually work:** I tend to edit several skills in one batch, and I forget to trigger things by hand. That's why automation (hooks, auto-triggering descriptions) matters more to me than remembering commands.
