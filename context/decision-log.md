# Decision Log

## How I Make Decisions

I try to analyze everything and talk it through with people, but my gut is usually where I end up either way. The analysis and conversations mostly serve to pressure-test the gut call, not replace it. When helping me decide, lay out the analysis, then ask what my gut says — and if they conflict, name the conflict directly instead of smoothing it over.

## What I Need Before Deciding

As much information as I can get, with timing at the top of the list: deadlines, when prices change, what else is happening that week, what I'd be giving up by committing now vs. later. A recommendation without a timing angle is incomplete for me.

## Typical Decisions

Nothing dramatic — the recurring types are:

- **Large purchases** — football tickets, flights. Cost vs. value, and when to buy.
- **Career tradeoffs** — interview scheduling, and whether to skip events or commitments to prep for an interview. Short-term cost (missing something) vs. long-term upside (a better interview).

## How I Handle Uncertainty

When I don't have enough information but still need to decide, I talk to other people and present my gut feeling to them. I want them to react to a position, not start from a blank slate.

## Who I Consult

Anyone I think has subject-matter knowledge on the specific decision. I welcome anything they give — information, pushback, or validation. I'm not looking for one type of input.
